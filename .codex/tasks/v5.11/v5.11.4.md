# UBOS v5.11.4 — Production-Safe Operational Analytics, Reporting, SLA Compliance, and Executive Dashboards

## Status

Planned

---

# Vision

Transform UBOS operational data into clear, trustworthy, role-specific intelligence for engineers, producers, executives, clients, and compliance teams.

UBOS should not only monitor individual productions in real time. It should also explain long-term performance across:

* productions
* channels
* operators
* sites
* platforms
* destinations
* customers
* workflows
* infrastructure
* reporting periods

The system must be able to answer:

```text
How reliable are our productions?

Which destinations fail most often?

Are we meeting contractual service levels?

What caused the largest operational losses?

Which productions consume the most resources?

How effective is automation?

How quickly do teams respond to incidents?

Where should we invest next?

What should executives know?
```

This phase converts observability, incident, audit, capacity, and production data into measurable business and operational outcomes.

---

# Strategic Purpose

Previous phases established:


UBOS v5.11.4 introduces the analytical and reporting layer that converts those capabilities into:

This phase makes UBOS suitable not only for control-room operation, but also for enterprise oversight, managed production services, broadcasters, production networks, and customer-facing service delivery.

# Analytics Philosophy

UBOS analytics must distinguish between:

```text
Raw Operational Data

↓

Normalized Measures

↓

Aggregated Metrics

↓

Calculated Indicators

↓

Business Interpretation
```

A dashboard should never silently convert incomplete or uncertain data into authoritative conclusions.

Every metric should expose:

* source
* time window
* aggregation method
* confidence
* data completeness
* calculation version

* Analytics recommendations cite supporting operational evidence.
