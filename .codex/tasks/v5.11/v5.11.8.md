# UBOS v5.11.8 — Production-Safe Multi-Tenant Organizations, Customer Isolation, Delegated Administration, and Service Management

## Status

Planned

---

# Vision

Transform UBOS from a single-organization broadcast platform into a secure, enterprise-grade, multi-tenant operating system capable of hosting multiple broadcasters, media companies, production houses, government agencies, universities, sports organizations, houses of worship, event companies, and managed-service customers on the same infrastructure without compromising security, isolation, performance, governance, or operational safety.

Every tenant must experience UBOS as if it were its own dedicated broadcast operating system.

UBOS must answer:

```text
Which organization owns this production?

Which customer owns this stream?

Can this operator access this production?

Which resources belong to which tenant?

Can two customers safely share infrastructure?

How are costs allocated?

Can a managed service provider administer customers without accessing their content?

Can enterprise customers manage themselves?

Can one customer never affect another?
```

This phase introduces the commercial operating model required for SaaS, enterprise licensing, cloud hosting, and managed broadcast services.

UBOS v5.11.8 must guarantee:

* no tenant can access another tenant's resources by default
* no cross-tenant analytics leakage
* no cross-tenant audit leakage
* no shared secret exposure
* no unauthorized delegated administration
* no support access without authorization
* no orphaned resources
* no privilege escalation across organizations
* no migration without integrity verification

# Acceptance Criteria

UBOS v5.11.8 is complete when:

* Multiple organizations can securely coexist on the same UBOS platform.
* Tenant isolation is enforced across identities, media, workflows, analytics, audit, APIs, AI services, and storage.
* Organizations support hierarchical structures including business units and workspaces.
* Every resource has deterministic ownership metadata.
* Authorization and audit are fully tenant-aware.
* Delegated administration supports platform, service-provider, organization, workspace, and production scopes.
* Customer onboarding and offboarding are automated and auditable.
* Tenant migration preserves operational continuity and historical records.
* Shared infrastructure never compromises logical isolation.
* Service catalogs and subscriptions define available platform capabilities.
* Quotas are enforceable with governed exceptions.
* Federation enables controlled cross-organization collaboration.
* Managed service providers can operate infrastructure without unrestricted customer-data access.
* Branding and white-label capabilities remain isolated per organization.
* Usage metadata supports future billing integrations.
* Multi-tenant operations remain fully compatible with governance, security, compliance, analytics, HA, and disaster recovery.
