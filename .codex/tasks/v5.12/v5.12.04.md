## Status

Planned

# Vision

UBOS v5.12.4 certifies that the Unified Broadcast Operating System can operate continuously, recover predictably, and maintain production safety during prolonged operation, unexpected failures, infrastructure instability, and mission-critical events.

This phase moves beyond functional correctness and performance.

Its objective is to prove operational resilience.

A professional broadcast platform must not simply perform well under ideal conditions—it must continue operating safely when the unexpected occurs.

UBOS must answer:

```text
Can UBOS operate continuously for weeks without degradation?

What happens when hardware fails during a live production?

Can the platform recover without losing production state?

Will Program output remain protected during failures?

Can operators continue working while subsystems recover?

Can automation resume correctly after interruptions?

Can enterprise customers trust UBOS for 24/7 operations?
```

# Reliability Invariants

UBOS v5.12.4 must guarantee:

* no single service failure causes uncontrolled platform failure
* no recovery bypasses security or governance controls
* no production state resumes without validation
* no failed extension destabilizes the control plane
* no recovery introduces cross-tenant data exposure
* no audit evidence is lost during failure or recovery
* no scheduler executes duplicated or omitted certified events after recovery
* no graceful degradation disables protected production capabilities
* no mission-critical certification passes without successful chaos and recovery validation

# Acceptance Criteria

UBOS v5.12.4 is complete when:

* Continuous soak testing validates stable long-running operation across supported deployment profiles.
* Controlled chaos engineering exercises are available for all critical subsystems.
* Representative infrastructure, platform, media, network, storage, database, and extension failures are successfully injected and recovered.
* Recovery plans define measurable RTO and RPO objectives with verification evidence.
* Production continuity prioritizes Program, audio, recording, and streaming during degradation.
* Workflow, scheduler, replay, recording, and automation state remain consistent after recovery.
* High Availability and Disaster Recovery scenarios are successfully exercised and certified.
* External dependency failures do not unnecessarily interrupt critical productions.
* Reliability metrics, scorecards, and availability calculations are continuously generated.
* Operators receive actionable recovery guidance throughout incidents.
* Mission-critical reliability evidence demonstrates predictable recovery, bounded degradation, and continuous operational integrity.
* The platform is certified to progress toward final GA readiness.
