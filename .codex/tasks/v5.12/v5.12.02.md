# UBOS v5.12.2 — Production-Safe Platform Performance Optimization, Resource Efficiency, Scalability, and Enterprise Load Certification

## Status

Planned

---

# Vision

UBOS v5.12.2 establishes the performance, efficiency, and scalability baseline required for enterprise General Availability.

The objective is to prove that the fully integrated UBOS platform can sustain realistic and extreme production workloads without unpredictable latency, uncontrolled resource growth, media interruption, cross-tenant interference, or operational instability.

This milestone does not pursue performance at the expense of production safety.

Optimization must preserve:

```text
Determinism

↓

Media Quality

↓

State Consistency

↓

Security

↓

Auditability

↓

Recoverability
```

UBOS must answer:

```text
How much production workload can this deployment support?

What is the maximum safe operating capacity?

Which subsystem becomes saturated first?

Does performance remain predictable under load?

Can multiple tenants operate simultaneously without interference?

Does automation remain timely during media-intensive production?

Can UBOS degrade safely rather than fail suddenly?

Are published hardware and deployment requirements trustworthy?
```

# Acceptance Criteria

UBOS v5.12.2 is complete when:

* Representative production and enterprise workload profiles are formally defined.
* Reproducible benchmark environments and load-generation tools are available.
* Video, audio, graphics, replay, recording, encoding, streaming, control, automation, security, governance, audit, API, and UI performance are measured.
* Frame-critical, interactive, operational, background, and batch workloads have distinct priorities and performance expectations.
* Performance baselines include percentile latency, throughput, resource use, and failure behavior.
* CPU, GPU, memory, storage, network, database, cache, queue, and connection usage remain bounded and observable.
* Long-running soak tests demonstrate no material memory, thread, handle, connection, subscription, or queue leaks.
* Scheduler, workflow, and rules systems maintain deterministic performance at certified scale.
* High command, event, API, social, automation, and telemetry loads do not destabilize live media.
* Multi-tenant tests prove isolation, quotas, fairness, and noisy-neighbor protection.
* Extension workloads remain sandboxed and resource-limited under stress.
* Background analytics, reporting, backup, AI, and maintenance workloads yield to production-critical workloads.
* Resource saturation produces visible warnings, controlled rejection, and graceful degradation rather than unpredictable failure.
* Degraded capabilities recover gradually and safely after pressure clears.
* HA, DR, backup, audit, security, and governance overhead are included in capacity certification.
* Vertical, horizontal, functional, tenant, and geographic scaling behavior is measured.
* Standard deployment tiers have published minimum and recommended requirements.
* Each certified deployment tier has a documented safe capacity envelope and required operational headroom.
* Performance regression gates prevent unacceptable degradation from entering later release candidates.
* Benchmark evidence is attributable, versioned, reproducible, and auditable.
* Operators can view live load, remaining headroom, bottlenecks, saturation risk, degradation state, and tenant impact.
* UBOS can sustain certified enterprise workloads without media interruption, state corruption, audit loss, security bypass, or tenant leakage.
