# UBOS v5.12.3 — Production-Safe Security, Tenant Isolation, Privacy, and Compliance Hardening Certification

## Status

Planned

---

# Vision

UBOS v5.12.3 establishes the final security, privacy, tenant-isolation, and compliance-hardening baseline required before Release Candidate progression and General Availability.

The objective is not merely to confirm that security features exist.

This milestone must prove that UBOS remains secure when:

* multiple tenants operate simultaneously
* privileged operators make mistakes
* plugins behave maliciously
* APIs are abused
* credentials are compromised
* services fail
* nodes lose connectivity
* recovery sites activate
* workloads reach saturation
* attackers deliberately target isolation boundaries

UBOS must answer:

```text
Can one tenant ever access another tenant’s data?

Can a plugin escape its sandbox?

Can an attacker bypass authorization through an alternate subsystem?

Can privacy obligations be enforced throughout the data lifecycle?

Can security controls survive failover and disaster recovery?

Can audit evidence prove that controls operated correctly?

Can enterprise customers trust UBOS with sensitive live productions?
```

# Acceptance Criteria

UBOS v5.12.3 is complete when:

* The platform security architecture and all major trust boundaries are formally reviewed.
* Threat models exist for every production-critical subsystem.
* Authentication, MFA, SSO, sessions, service identities, devices, and break-glass access are hardened and tested.
* Every production-changing path is confirmed to pass through authorization.
* Explicit deny, tenant scope, resource scope, locks, approvals, and policy restrictions resolve correctly.
* Cross-tenant access attempts fail across APIs, storage, databases, caches, queues, events, analytics, audit, backups, exports, AI, and support tools.
* Tenant context remains intact through asynchronous and cross-service execution.
* Plugins cannot escape sandboxes, obtain undeclared capabilities, access unauthorized networks, or cross tenant boundaries.
* APIs and WebSockets withstand malformed, forged, replayed, excessive, and unauthorized traffic.
* Device, node, and site identities are authenticated, revocable, and protected against stale control.
* Secrets are encrypted, scoped, rotated, redacted, and absent from logs, traces, exports, and crash artifacts.
* Encryption in transit and at rest is verified according to platform policy.
* Privacy controls support classification, minimization, retention, residency, consent, export, and deletion workflows.
* AI integrations enforce approved data scopes and provider policies.
* Audit records remain append-only, tenant-isolated, sequence-verifiable, hash-protected, and recoverable.
* Vulnerabilities are inventoried, prioritized, assigned, mitigated, patched, and verified.
* Release artifacts include signatures, provenance, dependency locks, SBOMs, and security-scan evidence.
* Security Operations can detect, correlate, contain, investigate, recover, and preserve evidence.
* Security controls remain active during overload, HA failover, DR activation, backup restoration, and degraded operation.
* Governance controls, evidence freshness, exceptions, and compliance results are validated.
* Independent penetration testing and remediation are completed or formally scheduled before GA.
* Critical and high release-blocking findings are resolved according to release policy.
* Security regressions automatically block later Release Candidate progression.
* A complete, tamper-evident security certification evidence package can be generated.
* The platform is certified for progression to the next GA hardening milestone.
