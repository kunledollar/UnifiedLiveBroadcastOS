# Vision

UBOS v5.12.10 is the final engineering, operational, commercial, and organizational approval milestone before the official General Availability (GA) release of the Unified Broadcast Operating System.

Unlike previous milestones that validated the product, this milestone validates the **release itself**.

The objective is to ensure that the exact Release Candidate approved in v5.12.9 is promoted to GA without introducing changes, while simultaneously confirming that the organization, infrastructure, support, documentation, launch operations, and post-launch response capabilities are fully prepared.

This milestone answers the final questions:

```text
Is this exact release approved for production use?

Can customers download it today?

Can support teams respond immediately?

Can launch operations handle global adoption?

Can critical incidents be managed after launch?

Is UBOS officially ready for General Availability?
```
