# Vision

UBOS v5.12.9 produces the first complete General Availability candidate of the Unified Broadcast Operating System.

All planned v5.x capabilities have now passed their major integration, workflow, performance, security, reliability, deployment, operator-experience, compatibility, customer-acceptance, and launch-readiness milestones.

The purpose of Release Candidate 1 is not to introduce new capabilities.

It is to freeze the intended GA product, expose it to final production-like operation, close remaining regressions, stabilize field deployments, and determine whether the exact candidate build is qualified for promotion to UBOS v6.0.0.

UBOS must answer:

```text
Can this exact build become the GA release?

Are all mandatory GA capabilities present and stable?

Do previously certified workflows still pass?

Have recent fixes introduced new regressions?

Can existing customers install and upgrade successfully?

Can the platform operate safely in real field environments?

Are all remaining defects understood?

Is there any reason this candidate must not be promoted?
```

# Acceptance Criteria

UBOS v5.12.9 is complete when:

* The first complete UBOS v6.0.0 Release Candidate is built from frozen, versioned, and verified source.
* The candidate has an exact manifest covering binaries, schemas, APIs, SDKs, plugins, deployment profiles, documentation, and known issues.
* All release artifacts are signed, hashed, provenance-backed, and matched across distribution channels.
* Feature, API, SDK, schema, plugin, UI terminology, documentation, and commercial packaging freezes are enforced.
* Only release-approved corrections can modify the candidate.
* Every candidate change creates a traceable change request and appropriate revalidation scope.
* The full mandatory regression portfolio passes against the exact candidate build.
* Previous v5.12 certifications are confirmed fresh or revalidated against RC1.
* RC1 operates successfully across representative standalone, professional, broadcast, enterprise, HA, DR, and multi-tenant environments.
* Field stabilization produces sufficient operating hours, production executions, and incident evidence.
* Long-running soak tests show bounded memory, queues, latency, storage growth, and scheduling drift.
* Every advertised installation profile passes with the final candidate artifacts.
* Every advertised upgrade path is rehearsed successfully.
* Supported rollback, restore, or roll-forward recovery procedures are verified and accurately documented.
* Critical security, authorization, privacy, tenant-isolation, audit, and supply-chain tests pass.
* Critical reliability, failover, recovery, backup, and disaster-recovery scenarios pass.
* Performance remains within approved certified limits.
* Support teams successfully handle simulated critical incidents using final diagnostics and documentation.
* Launch publication, monitoring, pause, advisory, rollback, and artifact-withdrawal procedures are rehearsed.
* All candidate defects are triaged into fix, known issue, restricted scope, or post-GA disposition.
* No unresolved critical blocker remains.
* Noncritical waivers are explicit, time-limited, approved, and customer-disclosed where required.
* The complete candidate evidence package is generated, protected, and reviewable.
* A formal promotion qualification recommends GA promotion, further stabilization, RC2 creation, or candidate rejection.
* The candidate is either approved for final GA certification or replaced by a new Release Candidate.
