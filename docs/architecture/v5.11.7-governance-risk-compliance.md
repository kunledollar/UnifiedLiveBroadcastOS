# UBOS v5.11.7 Governance, Risk, Compliance, and Policy Enforcement

## Architectural Ownership

The governance layer is owned by `@ubos/core` and implemented by `GovernanceRiskComplianceEngine`. It is metadata-only and does not run media clocks, schedulers, media-processing loops, or mutable UI state.

## Upstream Dependencies

- Observability health status and redaction utilities.
- Prior operational records from incident response, analytics, change governance, and security operations may be represented as evidence, risks, findings, obligations, and corrective actions.

## Downstream Consumers

- Production preflight and runtime governance gates.
- Compliance dashboards and reports.
- Audit, incident, change, security, customer, and executive workflows.

## Generation, Versioning, and Ownership

Policies are immutable by active version: registering an existing policy requires a strictly higher version. Activation is allowed only for approved or scheduled policy versions. Evidence identifiers are immutable and cannot be overwritten. Snapshots are cloned before publication, so callers cannot mutate engine-owned state.

## Policy and Control Model

Policies declare applicability, requirements, severity, and enforcement mode. Requirements map to governance controls, test results, and evidence requirements. Controls preserve implementation type, automation level, frequency, operational status, test IDs, and evidence sources.

## Compliance Evaluation and Enforcement

Evaluation deterministically sorts applicable policies by priority and ID. Each requirement evaluates control state, latest control test result, current evidence freshness, and active exceptions. Governance gates translate failed or partially compliant evaluations into allow, warn, acknowledgement, approval, or block decisions according to the policy enforcement mode. Risks above appetite require approval.

## Exceptions, Risks, and Corrective Actions

Exceptions are scoped to policy, requirement, subject, risk score, compensating controls, approver, and expiry. Expired exceptions are invalidated during evaluation. High-risk self-approved exceptions are rejected. Risks track inherent and residual scores separately, compare residual score against appetite, and can drive gate escalation. Corrective actions preserve owner, deadline, verification criteria, evidence, and lifecycle state.

## Frameworks, Obligations, and Audit Readiness

Compliance frameworks can map reusable UBOS controls to internal, customer, contractual, regulatory, or operational requirements. Obligations track notification, reporting, review, and remediation deadlines. The engine records metadata-only audit entries for policy, control, evidence, exception, risk, mapping, obligation, evaluation, and corrective-action changes.

## Failure Behavior

Unknown controls, unknown tests, stale policy versions, duplicate evidence IDs, high-risk self-approval, and non-approved activation fail closed with explicit errors. Missing, expired, invalid, or integrity-less evidence is never counted as proof of compliance.

## Security and Redaction

Names, descriptions, owners, approvers, evidence sources, references, metadata, and attestation statements are redacted through the shared observability redaction helper before storage in snapshots.

## Shutdown and Recovery

The implementation is in-memory for this phase and publishes deterministic snapshots suitable for persistence by later HA/DR phases. Restart recovery must restore policies, controls, evaluations, risks, exceptions, evidence references, obligations, and enforcement state before accepting production governance decisions.

## Validation

Focused validation covers policy versioning and activation, applicability, control mapping, evidence freshness and immutability, compliance scoring, exception approval and expiry, governance gates, framework mapping, risk appetite, corrective actions, obligations, bounded audit history, and redaction.
